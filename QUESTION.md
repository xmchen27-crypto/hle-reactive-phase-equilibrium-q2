# Question 2

A thermally insulated closed vessel is maintained at a constant pressure of $1.00\times10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$ and can perform only pressure-volume work. It contains $n=1.000\ \mathrm{mol}$ of a liquid consisting of solvent $A$ and two interconvertible molecular forms $B_0$ and $B_1$. The only reaction is $B_0\rightleftharpoons B_1$. The amounts of $A$ and $B_0+B_1$ are separately conserved; the overall fraction of $B_0+B_1$ is $z=0.1600$.
At this pressure, every homogeneous liquid phase is described by the following molar Gibbs energy, including its pure-component reference terms:
$$
g(T,\mathbf x)=C\left[(T-T_r)-T\ln\frac{T}{T_r}\right]
+x_1(\Delta h-T\Delta s)
+RT\left(x_A\ln x_A+x_0\ln x_0+x_1\ln x_1\right)
+W_{A0}x_Ax_0+W_{A1}x_Ax_1+W_{01}x_0x_1,




$$

where $x_A+x_0+x_1=1$, $x_0$ and $x_1$ refer to $B_0$ and $B_1$, and $0\ln0=0$. The temperature-independent parameters are
$$
T_r=300.00\ \mathrm K,\qquad C=80.00\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}},




$$

$$
\Delta h=-8000.0\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}},\qquad
\Delta s=-25.000\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}},




$$

$$
(W_{A0},W_{A1},W_{01})=(11000.0,6500.0,1600.0)\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}.




$$

Use $R=8.314462618\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. This is a complete idealized liquid-state model. All species have the same constant molar volume. No vapor or solid phases occur. Neglect interfacial energy and entropy and the heat capacity of the vessel. All liquid phases may exchange heat and material, and the reaction is allowed in every phase.
Initially, both reaction and macroscopic demixing have been suppressed. The sample is homogeneous at $300.00\ \mathrm K$, with $(x_A,x_0,x_1)=(0.8400,0.1600,0)$. These constraints are then released without supplying heat or performing non-pressure-volume work. Let the system reach its globally stable equilibrium, allowing liquid-liquid separation and disregarding kinetic barriers.
Determine the final temperature $T_f$, the fraction $\lambda_{\mathrm{rich}}$ of the total amount of substance contained in the liquid phase having the largest value of $b=x_0+x_1$, and the total entropy change $\Delta S=S_f-S_i$ of the $1.000\ \mathrm{mol}$ sample. If the final state is a single liquid phase, define $\lambda_{\mathrm{rich}}=1$. Establish global stability rather than accepting a merely stationary state.
Show your calculation and finish with the ordered triple $(T_f,\lambda_{\mathrm{rich}},\Delta S)$. Report temperature to two decimal places, phase fraction to four decimal places, and entropy change to three decimal places. Absolute tolerances are $0.02\ \mathrm K$, $0.0002$, and $0.002\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, respectively; all three entries must satisfy their tolerances. Units, in order: $\mathrm K$, dimensionless, $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
